AI Lead Intelligence & Outreach Automation System
Overview
An AI-powered lead qualification and outreach automation workflow designed to help businesses identify, research, qualify, and engage prospective customers.

Business problem 
Sales and business-development teams can spend significant time researching companies, assessing lead 
quality, preparing personalized outreach, and updating CRM records. Inconsistent qualification and incomplete 
research can also reduce outreach quality. 
Solution 
Designed an n8n workflow that retrieves new leads from Airtable, updates their processing status, researches 
company websites, transforms research into structured lead intelligence, categorizes leads according to 
qualification criteria, generates personalized outreach drafts for suitable leads, and sends drafts to a human 
reviewer through Telegram. Approved, rejected, and revision-requested outcomes are then recorded in 
Airtable. 

**Tools used**
● n8n 
● Airtable 
● Firecrawl 
● OpenAI models 
● Telegram 
● Structured-output parsers 
● Conditional routing 
● Human-in-the-loop approval 
Key features 
● Automated lead retrieval 
● Company website research 
● AI-assisted lead intelligence 
● Structured qualification output 
● Hot, warm, cold, and not-fit routing 
● Personalized outreach generation 
● Human approval through Telegram 
● AI-assisted revision 
● CRM status updates 
● Qualified and disqualified lead handling

**Business value** 
● Reduces repetitive lead research 
● Creates a consistent qualification process 
● Improves the relevance of outreach drafts 
● Keeps lead and outreach statuses visible 
● Maintains human control before outreach is approved 
● Creates a reusable foundation for sales automation


**Workflow Stages**
NEW ↓ RESEARCHING ↓ QUALIFIED ↓ READY FOR OUTREACH ↓ CONTACTED
